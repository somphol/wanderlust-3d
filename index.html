About the atlas

Built to be read
before you book.

Wanderlust 3D is a small, independent travel atlas. Every destination is chosen for a reason — a landscape, a kitchen, a way of moving through the day — and every entry is written to help you decide whether it belongs in your trip.

The photography is sourced from Unsplash under its free licence. Budgets are indicative daily averages for a mid-range traveller, covering a comfortable room, food, local transport and one paid activity. They are planning tools, not quotes.

Photography by Unsplash contributors

Seasons, not guesses

Each entry names the best window, the shoulder season and the trade-offs in between.

Honest daily budgets

Stay, food, transport and activities are broken out so you can see where the money goes.

A trip that remembers

Your shortlist and suggested nights are stored in this browser. No account, no tracking.
